Shengen Wu is currently an MPhil student at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)](https://hkust-gz.edu.cn). He is a member of The Deep Interdisciplinary Intelligence Lab (Di² Lab), working under the supervision of Professor [Yuetao Yue](https://facultyprofiles.hkust-gz.edu.cn/faculty-personal-page/YUE-Yutao/yutaoyue). His research interests focus on Large Language Models (LLMs), particularly in multimodal LLMs and reasoning enhancement in LLMs. Shengen Wu completed his Bachelor's degree in Science in 2024 at [Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University](xjtlu.edu.cn) and [University of Liverpool](liverpool.ac.uk), where he majored in Financial Mathematics.
