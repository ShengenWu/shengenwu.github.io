Shengen Wu is currently pursuing a Master of Philosophy (MPhil) degree at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)](https://hkust-gz.edu.cn). He is a member of The Deep Interdisciplinary Intelligence Lab (Di² Lab), advised by Professor [Yuetao Yue](https://facultyprofiles.hkust-gz.edu.cn/faculty-personal-page/YUE-Yutao/yutaoyue). His research focuses on Large Language Models (LLMs), with particular interests in multimodal learning and reasoning enhancement. He received his Bachelor of Science degree in Financial Mathematics from [Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en) and the [University of Liverpool](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/) in 2024.
